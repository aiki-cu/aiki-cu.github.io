Knowledge people can rely on

Universities, newsrooms, and publishers help people decide what to trust and which ideas to pursue. As AI becomes part of their work, we need evidence about what changes: who can contribute, how information is checked, and whose perspectives are represented.

AIKI brings together faculty in computer science and social research at CU Boulder. Philanthropic support can give people time to investigate these questions together and develop methods that others can examine and use.

What support could enable

Students working across fields
Help students contribute to research with center faculty, learn methods from more than one field, and spend sustained time on a question.
New research questions
Help faculty and students explore an idea, assess the evidence available, and determine what a larger study would require.
Shared tools and data
Support the preparation, evaluation, and documentation of research resources that can serve more than one investigation.

We can discuss a focused research interest or support for the center’s broader work, then develop a scope and budget through CU Boulder.

Building on CU Boulder’s commitment

AIKI was established on September 9, 2026, with $250,000 in inaugural funding from CU Boulder’s Department of Computer Science. Read the founding announcement.

Additional support can help develop the center’s research capacity. A conversation about your interests can identify a specific use for new funding and how progress would be assessed.
